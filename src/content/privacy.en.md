# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **La Patathèque** ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you visit **patatheque.ca**, contact us, sign up for our communications, make a reservation, place an order, or otherwise interact with our restaurant. If the French and English versions differ, the French version prevails.

## 1. Personal information we collect

We may collect the following personal information when you provide it voluntarily:

- **Name**;
- **Phone number**;
- **Email address**.

We may collect this information when you fill out a form, contact us, ask for information, sign up for our communications, make a reservation, or place an order.

Depending on the form, we also collect the details needed to handle your request: the items ordered and your notes, the date, time, and number of guests for a reservation or event and, if you apply for a job, your resume and your answers to the job's questions.

The site does not take payment online: you pay at the counter. We therefore do not collect any payment information through the site.

## 2. How we use personal information

We may use your name, phone number, and email address to:

- Answer your questions or requests;
- Contact you about a request, a reservation, or an order;
- Send you the communications you asked for;
- Send you restaurant news, offers, events, or promotions where the law allows it and we have obtained the required consent;
- Confirm or change your communication preferences;
- Manage reservations, orders, or service requests;
- Review your application, if you apply for a job;
- Protect our website, prevent misuse, and meet our legal obligations.

We will not use your personal information for purposes incompatible with those described here without obtaining the required consent or giving you the notice required by law.

## 3. Consent and marketing emails or messages

We collect and use your personal information with your knowledge and consent, except where applicable law allows another legal basis. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

If we send commercial electronic messages, we will follow applicable Canadian requirements, including identifying the sender, stating the purpose of the message, and providing a simple way to unsubscribe. You can unsubscribe from promotional communications at any time by using the unsubscribe link in the message or by contacting us at **patatheque@gmail.com**.

Unsubscribing from promotional communications may not stop essential or transactional communications, such as a reply to a request, a reservation confirmation, or information about an order.

## 4. Disclosure of personal information

We may disclose your personal information only when reasonably necessary to operate the restaurant and the website, including to:

- Reservation, ordering, email, or marketing service providers acting on our instructions;
- Website, hosting, security, or technical support providers, including our web host, Hostinger;
- Professional advisers or authorities where the law requires or allows it;
- An organization that succeeds us in a sale, merger, financing, or reorganization, with appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell your personal information.

Some service providers may process information outside Quebec. Before disclosing information to them, we assess whether it will be adequately protected there, and we put the disclosure under a written agreement. The information may then be subject to the laws of the place where it is processed.

## 5. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to maintain our business and consent records, resolve disputes, meet our legal obligations, and enforce our agreements. When it is no longer needed, we securely delete it or anonymize it where reasonably possible.

## 6. Safeguards

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards suited to the sensitivity of the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Cookies and analytics

We do not use cookies or analytics tools, other than technologies the website needs to work, such as remembering your language, your cart, and staff logins to the admin area.

The map on the "Contact" page and the site's fonts are provided by Google. Your browser sends your IP address to Google when they load, and Google may set its own cookies under its privacy policy.

If we add analytics or advertising tools, they will stay off until you accept them, and we will update this policy.

## 9. Children

Our website is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us using the details above.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices, services, or legal obligations change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
